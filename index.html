About OBF

Property is about people, progress and purpose.

Since 2015, OBF Property Investment Group has been building communities, transforming properties and creating opportunities across the Republic of Ireland. From hotels to homes, emergency housing to commercial units, we offer full-spectrum property solutions for private and public needs.

Based in Cork, we operate across Munster and Leinster — Cork City, Limerick City, Charleville, Mallow, Dublin City and more. Through our sister companies we run The Dutch Tulip Hotel in Mallow and the Bella Vista Hotel in Cobh, and we actively support government initiatives to tackle homelessness through emergency and short-term accommodation.
